Agent Portal — Access

Open the portal as an agent. Keep this page open as the customer.

  • The button above takes you to the portal agents use to take calls. In that portal, log in as an agent and switch your status to Available.

    On this site (the customer view), above and below this segment you’ll find options to generate a chat for the agent and to place a call to the IVR.

    For the IVR demo: place a call and press 9 to route to the next agent. If you set yourself to Available, you’ll receive your own call.

    For this demo, the phone number you use here has been linked to a customer profile called alex johnson, so you can interact as if you are this customer.

  • Tip: keep the portal open while calling from Phone Lab.

Requirements

  • Allow microphone/camera for WebRTC calls.
  • Disable aggressive blockers if the portal requires 3rd-party cookies or CORS flows.
  • Set your API_BASE and demo variables in this file.
